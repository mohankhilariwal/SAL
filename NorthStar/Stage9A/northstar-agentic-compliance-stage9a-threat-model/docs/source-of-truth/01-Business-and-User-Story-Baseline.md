# 01 Business and User Story Baseline - S09A Overlay

Maya Chen receives a regulatory publication whose body contains instruction-like text. Marcus Green asks whether the current system can prove that the text remains data rather than control, whether an integration can become a confused deputy, whether case memory or checkpoints can be poisoned, and whether a polished assessment can manipulate a reviewer. Priya Raman requires a system-specific threat model before identity and authorization are expanded.

Business acceptance for S09A is limited to repeatable threat discovery and control traceability. It does not certify production security, approve a model, deploy a control, activate a route, or transfer accountability from Daniel, Maya, Aisha, Sofia or Marcus.
