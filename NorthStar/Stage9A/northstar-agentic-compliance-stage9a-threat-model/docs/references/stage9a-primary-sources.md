## Source-status notes

- OWASP's agentic Top 10 is an industry/community framework, not a statutory standard.
- NIST SP 800-154 remains an initial public draft; it is used for general data-centric principles, not cited as a final standard.
- MCP security-best-practices material is draft/current guidance and must be reverified at implementation time.
- The current threat model maps to MITRE ATLAS conceptually; exact technique IDs are intentionally deferred until NorthStar has a production topology and red-team evidence.
