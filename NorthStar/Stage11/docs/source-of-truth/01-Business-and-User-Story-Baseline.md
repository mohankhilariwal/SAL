# 01 — Business and User Story Baseline

**Version:** `1.18.0`

NorthStar Financial Services operates in Canada, the United States and selected European markets. The accepted main user story remains:

> As a regulatory compliance analyst, Maya Chen wants an AI-assisted system to analyse a regulatory publication, identify obligations and affected units, policies and controls, generate an evidence-backed impact assessment and route high-risk findings for human approval, so that NorthStar responds faster without transferring accountability to AI.

The eight personas remain Maya Chen, Daniel Brooks, Priya Raman, Elena Petrov, Marcus Green, Sofia Alvarez, Liam O’Connor and Aisha Rahman.

## Final narrative state

The controlled local workflow can ingest, retrieve, analyse, use bounded tools, preserve state, collect evidence and request human review. The final architecture review concludes that the tutorial architecture is coherent but production evidence is insufficient. Daniel and Aisha do not authorize production. The agent remains an assistant within a human-accountable process.
