# 01 — Business and User Story Baseline

**Version:** `0.5.0`

## Context carried from S02B

Maya can retrieve exact authorized evidence for candidate lending, payments and customer-data impacts. The output remains evidence, not an accepted obligation, policy mapping, control gap or case decision.

## S03A narrative state

Maya must otherwise leave the application to search a regulatory catalogue, query control records, create a draft case, save a candidate mapping and queue human review. Priya and Marcus require those operations to become controlled capabilities before any model is allowed to choose among them. Elena implements strict descriptors and one gateway; Sofia requires negative permission and disposition tests.

## Business outcome of S03A

NorthStar now has a deterministic capability boundary for six local tutorial operations. A conventional application caller can invoke them safely enough to demonstrate contracts, policy ordering, idempotency and failure semantics. The stage does not automate goal pursuit.

## Non-goals

No live regulatory monitoring, enterprise control service, accepted `DATA-002 RegulatoryCase`, `DATA-007 ReviewDecision`, agent loop, graph, memory, MCP/A2A, external notification, final impact assessment or remediation execution.
