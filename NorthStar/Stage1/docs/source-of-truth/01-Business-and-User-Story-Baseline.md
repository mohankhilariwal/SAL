# 01 - Business and User Story Baseline

**Version:** 0.2.0

## Organization and primary story

NorthStar Financial Services operates in Canada, the United States and selected European markets across banking, payments, lending and insurance-related services.

`US-001` remains the primary story: as a regulatory compliance analyst, Maya wants AI assistance to analyse a new publication, identify obligations and candidate impacts, create evidence-backed outputs and route high-risk findings for human approval without transferring accountability to AI.

## Supporting user stories retained

- `US-002` Daniel Brooks reviews and approves high-risk findings.
- `US-003` Priya Raman needs explicit, bounded and traceable architecture behavior.
- `US-004` Elena Petrov needs portable model/runtime integration and measurable performance.
- `US-005` Marcus Green needs least privilege, input distrust and controlled blast radius.
- `US-006` Sofia Alvarez needs versioned evaluation, governance and model-risk evidence.
- `US-007` Liam O'Connor needs observable, recoverable and supportable operation.
- `US-008` Aisha Rahman needs evidence-backed candidate impacts separated from accepted control/process decisions.
- `US-009` Maya needs preserved source provenance and reproducible evidence.
- `US-010` NorthStar data owners need authorized access to internal knowledge and records.
- `US-011` workflow owners need explicit state, termination, escalation and recovery.
- `US-012` executives and assurance functions need concise approved reporting and audit evidence.

## Stage 1 narrative state

Maya received an urgent synthetic publication mentioning automated credit adjudication, payment screening and customer-data sharing. Priya selected the smallest useful capability: a source-bounded preliminary summarizer. It helps with the first reading but does not determine NorthStar applicability.

## Stage 1 business outcome

The system can reduce the formatting and extraction burden for one supplied publication while preserving human accountability. It cannot answer Maya's next question: which NorthStar policies, controls, processes or prior assessments support the candidate affected areas.

## Next opening condition

Stage 2 begins when Daniel asks for internal evidence behind the summary. The unresolved need is authorized grounded retrieval, not increased autonomy.
