# 00 — Project Constitution, Stage 10C Overlay

Version `1.17.0`. Preserve all prior constitutional items.

New invariants:

1. FinOps optimizes value per safe completed business outcome, not tokens alone.
2. Every cost, capacity, SLO, RTO/RPO and readiness artefact has `authority_effect: none`.
3. Cost controls cannot suppress authorization, policy, security, audit, integrity, reconciliation, approval or mandatory evidence.
4. Ordinary reliability error budget is separate from a zero-tolerance control gate.
5. Capacity assumptions must expose workload distribution, peak, utilization and headroom.
6. One concurrent protected write remains the maximum.
7. RTO/RPO are business-owned and remain proposals until approved and exercised.
8. Production readiness evidence cannot activate a route.
9. Stage 8D and Stage 9D remain explicit hard blockers.
10. Illustrative rates and targets must not be represented as provider prices or production commitments.
