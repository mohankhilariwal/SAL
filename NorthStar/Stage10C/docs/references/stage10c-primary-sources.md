# Stage 10C Primary Sources

Verified on 2026-08-01. The Stage 10C implementation remains vendor-neutral; these sources support general engineering practices rather than vendor selection. Rapidly changing specifications were checked against their current official pages on that date.

- `[R1]` FinOps Foundation, **FinOps Framework 2026 / Framework Overview** — FinOps as an operational framework and collaborative practice for maximizing technology value. https://www.finops.org/framework/ and https://www.finops.org/insights/2026-finops-framework/
- `[R2]` FinOps Foundation, **Capability: Unit Economics** — linking technology cost to business and customer outcome units. https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/unit-economics/
- `[R3]` FinOps Foundation, **Token Economics: The Atomic Unit of AI Value** — AI unit economics and the limits of list-price-only optimization. https://www.finops.org/insights/token-economics-the-atomic-unit-of-ai-value/
- `[R4]` FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification, **FOCUS Specification v1.4** — current common technology billing schema and terminology; v1.4 was announced in June 2026. https://focus.finops.org/focus-specification/ and https://focus.finops.org/focus-specification/changelog/
- `[R5]` Google SRE, **Introduction / Capacity Planning** and **Production Services Best Practices** — demand forecasts, load testing and empirical resource-to-service capacity. https://sre.google/sre-book/introduction/ and https://sre.google/sre-book/service-best-practices/
- `[R6]` Kubernetes Documentation, **Horizontal Pod Autoscaling** — controller-based workload scaling. https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/autoscaling/horizontal-pod-autoscale/
- `[R7]` Kubernetes Documentation, **Resource Management for Pods and Containers** — requests, limits and scheduling behavior. https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/configuration/manage-resources-containers/
- `[R8]` Google SRE, **Reliable Product Launches** and **Testing Reliability** — load and stress testing for launch and capacity confidence. https://sre.google/sre-book/reliable-product-launches/ and https://sre.google/sre-book/testing-reliability/
- `[R9]` Google SRE Workbook, **Implementing SLOs** — user-relevant SLIs, SLOs and error budgets. https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/
- `[R10]` Google SRE Workbook, **Error Budget Policy for Service Reliability** — error budgets as a change and reliability control mechanism. https://sre.google/workbook/error-budget-policy/
- `[R11]` OpenTelemetry, **GenAI semantic conventions** and **Inside the LLM Call: GenAI Observability** — usage and token telemetry for operational and cost analysis; the conventions remain under active development. https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/registry/attributes/gen-ai/ and https://opentelemetry.io/blog/2026/genai-observability/
- `[R12]` NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1, **Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems** — business impact analysis, recovery strategy, RTO and RPO concepts. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final

No vendor price, benchmark or product-availability claim is derived from these sources. Rate-card values in the repository are synthetic configuration assumptions.
