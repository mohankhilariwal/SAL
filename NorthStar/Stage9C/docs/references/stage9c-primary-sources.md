# Stage 9C Primary Sources

Verified 2026-08-01. Vendor-neutral architecture remains primary; product mappings are examples, not mandatory selections.

1. **NorthStar Stage 9B Handoff Pack** — authoritative `1.13.0` compatibility constraints and next problem.
2. **Narrative-Driven Agentic AI Architecture Playbook** — Stage U/V requirements, continuous repository and chapter format.
3. **NIST AI RMF 1.0 and NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile** — risk governance, lifecycle and GenAI profile considerations.
   - https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
4. **NIST AI RMF Playbook / Govern** — suggested actions for Govern, Map, Measure and Manage and governance documentation.
   - https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/
   - https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/
5. **Open Policy Agent official documentation** — policy bundles, decision logs, status and external data/cache patterns.
   - https://openpolicyagent.org/docs/management-bundles
   - https://openpolicyagent.org/docs/management-decision-logs
   - https://openpolicyagent.org/docs/management-status
   - https://openpolicyagent.org/docs/external-data
6. **Cedar official documentation** — schema and policy validation principles.
   - https://docs.cedarpolicy.com/schema/schema.html
   - https://docs.cedarpolicy.com/policies/validation.html
7. **OWASP AI Agent Security and Prompt Injection guidance** — defense in depth, least privilege and human approval for destructive/high-impact actions.
   - https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/AI_Agent_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html
   - https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html
   - https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/
8. **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** — AI management system requirements and continual improvement overview.
   - https://www.iso.org/standard/42001
9. **ISO/IEC 23894:2023** — AI risk management guidance overview.
   - https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

No source is used to claim that the local Stage 9C package is certified, legally compliant, production-ready or a standards-conformant deployment of any named policy engine.
