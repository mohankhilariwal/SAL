## Attack tree root: cause an ungoverned NorthStar effect

1. Manipulate content to change plan.
2. Bypass or misplace a PEP.
3. Tamper with policy or cache.
4. Abuse a policy exception.
5. Forge or reuse human review.
6. Exploit central outage/staleness.
7. Compromise policy administrator.
8. Convert a guardrail allow into authority.

Each branch maps to one or more hard controls and negative tests. No threat-model output can deploy a treatment, alter policy, approve/finalize or mutate protected state.
