# TM-001/1.3.0 — Stage 10A Observability and Audit Delta

This delta extends `TM-001/1.2.0` without changing accepted authority owners. Telemetry and audit evidence have `authority_effect: none` and cannot authorize tools, approve/finalize cases, activate routes, issue grants or mutate `DATA-106`.

## Threat-model conclusion

The local design detects accidental and unsophisticated tampering and proves ordered integrity under a locally protected HMAC key. It does not provide independent third-party non-repudiation, WORM guarantees, trusted time, multi-region durability or legal admissibility. Those remain production obligations after Stage 9D and Stage 8D are resolved.
