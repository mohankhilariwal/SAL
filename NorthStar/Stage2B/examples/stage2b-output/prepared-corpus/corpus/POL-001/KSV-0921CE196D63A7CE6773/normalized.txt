# Responsible Lending Policy

## Affordability and ability to repay
NorthStar must assess a borrower's ability to repay before approving or materially increasing a lending facility.
The assessment must consider verified income, committed expenses, existing debt obligations and reasonably foreseeable payment stress.
An automated recommendation may support the analysis, but it must not replace the accountable lending decision.
Evidence of the assessment and any exception must be retained with the application record.

## Vulnerable customers and adverse outcomes
Analysts must identify indicators of financial vulnerability and avoid practices that could create unfair or unsustainable outcomes.
Material policy exceptions require named human review and documented rationale.
