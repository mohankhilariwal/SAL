# Regulatory Change Taxonomy

## Lending
Lending topics include affordability, ability to repay, creditworthiness, vulnerable customers, collections and fair treatment.

## Payments
Payments topics include sanctions screening, restricted parties, transaction monitoring, fraud, settlement and operational resilience.

## Customer data
Customer-data topics include personal information, privacy, consent, third-party sharing, cross-border transfer, retention and data minimization.

## Evidence and authority
A taxonomy label supports classification and retrieval; it is not an authoritative policy obligation or final legal interpretation.
