# Payments Screening and Escalation Process

## Pre-execution screening
Payments must be screened against applicable sanctions, restricted-party and high-risk jurisdiction rules before release.
Potential matches are held for review; the process must not silently bypass a screening result.

## Transaction monitoring
Post-execution transaction monitoring identifies unusual patterns, structuring indicators and repeated threshold avoidance.
A material alert is escalated to Payments Compliance with transaction evidence, customer context and the screening disposition.

## Operational recovery
If the screening service is unavailable, the payment remains queued or follows the approved manual contingency; it is not automatically released.
