# 01 — Business and User Story Baseline — Stage 7B overlay

All accepted NorthStar personas and `US-001`–`012` remain unchanged.

## Operational scenarios added

- Maya requires responsive short regulatory questions during analysis.
- Long publications and cross-policy comparisons may run asynchronously without blocking interactive work.
- Overnight regulatory batches require a completion window and fair admission rather than interactive TTFT.
- Elena needs profile-specific load evidence before selecting inference scale.
- Liam needs queue, latency, token and external-dependency measurements tied to trace/run identifiers.
- Sofia requires evidence classification, profile provenance and change controls before a capacity recommendation is applied.

These scenarios introduce measurement and planning only. They do not change business accountability or human approval ownership.
