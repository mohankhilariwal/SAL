# 01 — Business and User Story Baseline (Reconstructed 1.6.0 Overlay)

## Retained baseline

The organization, personas, main regulatory-impact user story and supporting user stories `US-001`–`012` remain unchanged. Human accountability is not transferred to AI.

## S07A narrative increment

After obligation extraction creates an immutable evidence package, Maya Chen needs independent jurisdiction, evidence and policy-mapping work to finish faster. Priya Raman introduces concurrency only for branches that do not depend on one another and cannot change protected state or exercise human authority. Elena Petrov owns the local runtime implementation, Marcus Green owns concurrency threat controls, Sofia Alvarez owns governed policy and evaluation evidence, and Liam O’Connor owns queue/worker telemetry and recovery operations.

## Business outcomes sought

- Reduce wall-clock time for independent analysis without increasing autonomous authority.
- Preserve evidence completeness and deterministic aggregation.
- Prevent duplicate work, overload and one-case monopolization.
- Retain sequential execution for dependent or high-impact actions.

## Out of scope

Concurrent agents, concurrent writes, automatic approval, shared mutable memory, live production brokers, multi-region execution, production IAM and capacity planning are not delivered in S07A.
