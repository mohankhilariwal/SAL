# Automated Lending Decision Policy

## Purpose
NorthStar requires documented human accountability for material credit decisions and for changes to automated adjudication rules.

## Decision evidence
Every material automated lending decision must retain the decision date, decision outcome, model or rule version, principal factors, and reviewer identity when a manual override occurs.

## Change control
Changes to lending decision logic require testing, policy-owner approval, and a rollback plan before production release.

## Review
Compliance reviews this policy annually and when a relevant regulatory requirement changes.
