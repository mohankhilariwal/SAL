# Customer Data Sharing Control

## Objective
Prevent unauthorized disclosure or secondary use of customer information.

## Control activities
Data-sharing integrations must enforce approved purpose, recipient, jurisdiction, classification, retention period, and revocation requirements.

Evidence must include the approved data-sharing request, authorization result, transferred data categories, transfer timestamp, destination, and exception records.

## Escalation
Any transfer outside the approved purpose or geography is escalated to the privacy and cybersecurity owners.
